Download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/qubodup-wet_snow_steps.7z . Converted from the nine supplied FLAC files to mono48kHz PCM WAV, normalized with FFmpeg loudnorm I=-20/TP=-3/LRA=7. These are the user-selected snow recordings used as basic placeholder footsteps on Dust2, not a sand/stone recording or a material-dependent sound bank.

HRTF and native gain/filter handle playback; no new audio scene is constructed.
